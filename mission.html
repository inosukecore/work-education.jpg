Our Mission

DIGNITY IS
NON-NEGOTIABLE.

We exist to ensure that no woman in India is held back by period poverty, silence, or stigma. Our mission is to deliver menstrual health, dignity, and opportunity to the communities that need it most — together with the women who lead them.

End period poverty

Free pads, period education, and access — at scale.

Break the stigma

Open conversation in homes, schools, and communities.

Build self-reliance

Skilling, mentorship, and grassroots leadership.

Center women's voices

100% women-led. Always.
