About Us

EMPOWERING WOMEN
THROUGH CHANGE.

Queens Of Change Foundation is dedicated to menstrual health, dignity, and equal opportunity for underprivileged women and girls across India.

Through grassroots outreach, health education, and empowerment programs, we work directly with communities to build confidence, wellbeing, and long-term self-reliance.

  • ✓Menstrual health & dignity programs
  • ✓500+ communities reached across India
  • ✓100% women-led organization
  • ✓Education that breaks the silence

How We Built This Journey

FROM LOCAL ACTION
TO NATIONWIDE IMPACT.

Phase 1

Starting With Menstrual Dignity

We began by addressing period stigma and access — reaching underprivileged women and girls with awareness and essential menstrual care support.

Phase 2

Expanding Health Education

Our team expanded community education on menstrual health, hygiene, and reproductive rights to break silence and build informed communities.

Phase 3

Scaling Outreach Across India

Through local partnerships and field outreach, we expanded into 500+ communities and increased access where it is needed most.

Phase 4

Women-Led Impact At Scale

As a 100% women-led organization, we have distributed 2,00,000+ sanitary pads and directly impacted 50,000+ lives across India.
